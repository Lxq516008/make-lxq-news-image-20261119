# The news in the image — Thursday 19 November 2026

Every story on the front page was checked against reporting of 19 November
2026. Only events that happened, or were first reported, on that day are
shown.

1. **Shenzhen summit** — Trump, Xi and Putin met face to face in Shenzhen on
   the sidelines of APEC, the first-ever trilateral summit of the three
   leaders. Their joint declaration backed UN reform and more oil output;
   there was no breakthrough on Ukraine or Gaza. (The Times live blog; AP
   via Bangladesh Chronicle; EGazette)
2. **ICJ ruling on Myanmar** — The International Court of Justice ruled that
   Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, the first ruling of its
   kind by the UN's highest court. (Just Security Early Edition, 19 Nov;
   AppiSToS)
3. **Gaza** — Israel launched a rare ground operation in part of central Gaza,
   with tanks crossing the ceasefire line near Nuseirat after evacuation
   orders; Hamas called it a violation. (Just Security; UBStandard)
4. **Ukraine** — Russia said Ukraine attacked the Crimean Bridge for the
   third time in a week; Russian strikes cut power in the Dnipropetrovsk,
   Zhytomyr, Zaporizhzhia, Mykolaiv and Sumy regions. (Just Security;
   EGazette; UBStandard)
5. **US Senate** — The Senate rejected the Iran war-powers resolution 51–48.
   Trump said the US would "see what happens" in the Iran war after
   1 January 2027. (AppiSToS; Just Security)
6. **Anthropic IPO** — Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share on 18
   November (above the $70–$78 range), sold 980 million shares for $81.34
   billion — the largest IPO on record — and ANT started trading on Nasdaq
   on 19 November. (Reuters/Bloomberg via enjoy.dev; BlockWest; SEC filings)
7. **Florida floods** — Heavy rain sent the St. Johns River past 18 feet at
   Cocoa, its highest ever (the old record was 17.6 ft in 1953); schools
   closed across the Space Coast. (Florida Today)

The image itself is drawn, not photographed: `generate_image.py` renders the
front page with flat shapes and text (Python + Pillow), so every label is
crisp and legible and nothing in it is a borrowed photograph or artwork.
